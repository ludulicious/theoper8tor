ABOUT


What sets Build.Inc apart is our care for each client that walks in the door. Our staff gets to know you personally, and they're there to guide you towards the best results for you. Our goal is to constantly build you to the next level of health and wellness.


We were founded on the idea that fitness studios are the foundation of the modern community. We bring people together through shared fitness and vitality goals. This social aspect permeates the entire studio from our friendly personal trainers and staff to group weight loss programs to the communal setup around the pool and main floor.


It's easier to make a change and upgrade your life with a team on your side. Build.Inc is your team to build to the next level.

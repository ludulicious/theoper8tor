#work-through-one-priority

An offer that needs shaping. A funnel that needs direction. A social media or marketing strategy that needs to be laid out. Choose one focus and use the session to work through it with Staci.

#what-is-included

- A one-hour consultation and a 30-minute follow-up.
- A recording of your session.
- Recommended strategies, tools and experts relevant to your focus.
- A resource listing to support your next steps.

#is-this-the-right-session

Choose Fast Action when you know what you want to work on. If you have several ideas and need to decide between them, [Brilliance — Brain Dump — Bank](/book-a-session/brilliance-brain-dump-bank) gives you space to sort your options.

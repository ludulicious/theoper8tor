#see-how-the-whole-journey-connects

Work through one funnel from first interest to becoming a client. We will determine your offer suite and map the pages, emails and communications that connect each step.

- Visual mapping of one complete client journey.
- Planning for your offer suite, pages, emails and other communications.
- Templates and referrals for copy and content.
- Email marketing platform referrals.
- Your session recording, recommended strategies, tools and experts, and a resource listing.

#planning-before-building

This is the map, blueprint and framework. **Technical funnel building is not included.** Referrals can be made for technical services when you are ready to implement.

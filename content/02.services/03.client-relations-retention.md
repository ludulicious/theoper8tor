#look-at-the-experience-from-the-clients

We will walk through how you welcome, communicate with and say goodbye to clients. The focus is one area of opportunity in your client relations and retention process.

#what-we-will-review

- Onboarding and your client relationship management (CRM) process.
- Email marketing and other methods of communication.
- Offboarding and exit interviews.

#what-is-included

A 90-minute session, a 15-minute follow-up, your session recording, recommended strategies, tools and experts, and a resource listing.

For a broader review of your systems and tools, [explore the Systems Audit](/book-a-session/systems-audit).

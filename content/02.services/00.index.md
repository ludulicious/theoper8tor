# Strategy for your offers, clients and systems.

You have ideas for your business, but deciding what to do next can be harder than coming up with them. I help you sort the options, work through the gaps and connect the next steps.

## What do you want to work on?

### Turn your ideas into an offer and a plan

You have skills, knowledge or several possible directions, but need to decide where to focus. We can sort what you already have, think through your audience and shape an offer or marketing strategy.

This work helps you decide which idea to pursue and what to do next.

### Improve the experience your clients have with you

Winning a client is only the beginning. We can review how you welcome clients, communicate with them and finish the engagement, looking for opportunities to improve the relationship and encourage retention.

The focus is your actual process: onboarding, CRM, email communication, offboarding and feedback.

### Connect your systems and client journey

Your tools and processes should support the way you want to work. We can review your existing systems or map the journey from first interest to becoming a client, including your offers, pages, emails and communications.

The work is review and planning. Where you need technical implementation or specialist support, relevant referrals can be discussed.

## Retention is Essential

It's no surprise that it wasn't as simple as just getting them to say yes. The art of keeping clients is a marathon, not a sprint.

If you've done your research you know that it is cheaper to keep your existing clients than to take on new ones.

If you are just getting started and trying to keep your costs down, you should retain your current clients and use their unparalleled experiences to get word-of-mouth referrals. And if your prospects came from paid ads, You'll need to put a little time in and convert them to warm clients.

I've put together a few ides to help you "keep your clients".

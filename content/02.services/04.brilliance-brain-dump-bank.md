- Your options for audience targeting and additional lead generation.

A 90-minute strategy session and two 15-minute progress checks. You will also receive your session recording, recommended strategies, tools and experts, and a resource listing.

The focus is choosing a direction and planning your next steps, using what you already know and have available.

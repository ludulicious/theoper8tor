## What the programme covers

### Your starting point

Your audience, branding, copy, product or service and client journey. These form the intake across packages.

### Your relationships

Where to find people, how to attract and retain them, compensation, perks and benefits, and when to end a relationship.

### Your process

Platform guidelines, weekly meetings and a training platform, depending on the package. Discuss the current package scope and availability before booking.

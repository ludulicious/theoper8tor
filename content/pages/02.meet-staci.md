My work brings together strategy, resources and relationships to help entrepreneurs, speakers, authors and trainers find their next step.

## A background in people and business

My experience spans customer service, public relations, retention and sales, supported by a background in behavioural and social sciences.

As founder of Meetup Moguls and owner of A Marketer’s Lounge, I connect people with resources, professionals and opportunities for collaboration. I also work as a speaker, trainer, moderator, event host and producer.

## How that shapes our work

We start with your concerns and what you already have. From there, we look at your options, your client experience and the systems that support your business. Where it helps, I connect you with tools and specialists relevant to the work.

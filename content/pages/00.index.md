## Your ideas. My perspective and connections.

My background spans customer service, public relations, retention and sales. As the founder of Meetup Moguls and owner of A Marketer’s Lounge, connecting people with relevant resources is at the heart of my work.

I work with entrepreneurs, speakers, authors and trainers to look at what they already have, what they need and which next steps make sense.

#introduction

Get a fresh perspective on your offers, client relationships and business systems. I’m Staci Moore, The Oper8tor. I help you sort the options and connect you to the resources and relationships to move forward.

#where-are-you-right-now

Start with a conversation, tackle a specific challenge or give your ideas a direction. These three sessions are a good place to begin.

#your-ideas-my-perspective-and-connections

My background spans customer service, public relations, retention and sales. As the founder of Meetup Moguls and owner of A Marketer’s Lounge, connecting people with relevant resources is at the heart of my work.

I work with entrepreneurs, speakers, authors and trainers to look at what they already have, what they need and which next steps make sense.

#what-working-together-looks-like

1. **Choose your focus.** Find the session that fits your current challenge.
2. **Work through it together.** Bring your ideas, questions and existing processes.
3. **Plan your next steps.** Each session’s page explains its scope, resources and follow-ups.

Not sure what you need? Start with the [Initial Consultation](/book-a-session/initial-consultation).
